How verdicts work

PawWeather combines two separate risks, judged for your dog's profile:

  • Paws — estimated pavement temperature, from National Weather Service data, sun angle, cloud cover, and surface type (asphalt or concrete).
  • Heat — the humidity-aware heat index, with stricter thresholds for flat-faced breeds, puppies, seniors, and dark coats in sun.

Pavement temperatures are estimates. The hand test always has the final say: press the back of your hand on the pavement for 7 seconds — if it's too hot for you, it's too hot for their paws. PawWeather is an educational planning tool, not veterinary advice.

Common questions

  • Where does the weather come from? The National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), a free U.S. public service. Coverage is currently U.S. only.
  • What does Pro include? The 7-day walk planner, smart alerts, and profiles for up to 6 dogs. One-time purchase — no subscription, no ads.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore purchases (Pro is tied to your Apple ID).
  • Night suggestions don't work for my area: Settings → Walk hours lets you cap the earliest and latest times PawWeather will ever suggest.
