Before you go

The 7-second test beats any calculator

Press the back of your hand on the pavement for 7 seconds. If you can't hold it, it's too hot for paws. These temperatures are estimates from air temperature, sun and sky — your street may differ.

After sunset, asphalt stays roughly 7 °F warmer than the air for hours — we checked 16 road sensors in Arizona at 11 p.m. and every single one was still warmer than the air. The full data is here.
