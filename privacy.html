Privacy

Paw Weather Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

Paw Weather collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account (see iCloud backup), except the town name you type for a trip, which is looked up by Apple (see Location). The developer never receives any of it.

Location

If you allow location access ("While Using the App"), your coordinates are sent to a public weather service to fetch the local forecast: the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) in the United States, and MET Norway (api.met.no) everywhere else. Apart from that, your location is only ever handled by Apple's own iOS services: to show the map, to look up the name of the place outside the United States, and to make a walk course when you ask for one (see below). We never store, log, or share your location, and the app works without it — you can pick a city manually instead.

Walk routes. When you start a walk in the app, it records your route so the map can show which parts of the walk were GO, CAUTION or DANGER. This uses the location background mode only between "Start walk" and "End walk", so the route keeps recording if your screen locks mid-walk; it stops as soon as the walk ends. Routes are stored on your phone (and in your own iCloud backup, if it is on). They are never sent to the developer or anyone else, and you can delete any walk from the walk list.

Rain by the minute (Apple Weather). During a walk only, while the walk screen is open, the app asks Apple WeatherKit (built into iOS) for the next hour's minute-by-minute rain at your current position, at most once every 15 minutes. Only the coordinates are sent, only to Apple, and only for that request; nothing is sent when you are not walking. We never receive, store or share them. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Weather data from Apple Weather.

Walk courses. When you tap "Choose a course", the app asks Apple Maps (MapKit, built into iOS) for nearby parks around your start point, and then for walking directions between your start point and the parks it picked as waypoints. Only those points are sent, only at that moment, and only to Apple — never to us. Your recorded walk routes are never sent. "Usual routes" are worked out on your phone from your own past walks and are not sent anywhere. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Trip destinations. When you add a trip and type a town or city, that text is sent to Apple's geocoding service (built into iOS) to find the place. The app does not attach your location to that request. From a week before the trip, and only while you have that trip open, the destination's coordinates are sent to the same public weather services (NWS or MET Norway) to fetch the forecast there. The destination is saved with the plan on your phone only.

Your dog's profile & settings

Names, photos, breed details, and preferences are stored on your device. Care records, walks, condition logs, weight, medicines, care plans (including trip destinations and packing lists), memories and diary entries are stored the same way.

iCloud backup of your records

If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, Paw Weather keeps a copy of your records and photos in your own iCloud Drive, in a private folder that only this app uses (it doesn't appear in the Files app). This is how your records move to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. The copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; the developer has no access to it and receives nothing. You can turn it off for Paw Weather in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud, and manage or delete it there.

You can also save a backup file yourself (Settings → Back up records). The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as AirDrop or the Files app.

Purchases

Paw Weather Pro is offered three ways: a monthly subscription with a free trial, a yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple ID settings. Prices are shown in the App Store. If you started the 14-day free trial offered in version 1.4.0, it keeps working until it ends; it was never charged automatically.

All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Purchases can be restored on the same Apple ID from Settings → Restore purchases.

Notifications

Alerts, care plan reminders (vet visits, vaccines, preventives, grooming, pet hotel stays and trips), medicine reminders, cold-night alerts, the walk-length reminder and the reminder before a 1.4.0 free trial ends are all scheduled locally on your device. No push servers are involved.

Third parties

No analytics, no ad networks, no tracking SDKs — nothing in this app reports back to us. The app connects to the two public weather services above, to Apple Maps (MapKit) for the walk map and — only when you ask for a walk course — for park search and walking directions, and to Apple's geocoding service when you type a trip destination. Purchases go through Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details.

This website

This website (not the app) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and collects no personally identifiable information. The app itself still sends nothing.

Changes & contact
